About Us

Last updated: 13 October 2026

HMI Fashion Store is an online fashion store operated by Parvez Fashion Store, based in Sitarganj, Uttarakhand. We sell ethnic and western wear for women, men and kids — sarees, suits, kurtis, lehengas, jackets, t-shirts and more — and deliver across India.

What we offer

  • Carefully selected clothing at fair prices, with all prices shown in Indian Rupees (₹) inclusive of applicable taxes.
  • Secure online payments through Razorpay (UPI, cards, net banking, wallets) and Cash on Delivery on eligible products.
  • Order tracking from your account under “My Orders”.
  • Easy returns and exchanges within 7 days of delivery — see our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
