Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains how Parvez Fashion Store (“we”, “us”), which runs the HMI Fashion Store website at shop.hmitechnology.co.in, collects, uses and protects your personal information. By using our website you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your mobile number (when you log in with OTP), or your name and email address (when you log in with Google).
  • Order details: name, delivery address, phone numbers, the products you order and the order amount.
  • Payment details: payments are processed by Razorpay. We receive only the payment status and a payment reference. We never see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank login details.
  • Wishlist and saved addresses that you choose to save in your account.
  • Device storage: your cart and wishlist may be stored in your browser so they are not lost when you refresh the page.

2. How we use your information

  • To process, ship and deliver your orders and to handle returns, exchanges and refunds.
  • To contact you about your order by phone, SMS, WhatsApp or email.
  • To prevent fraud and keep our website secure.
  • To meet our legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who we share it with

  • Courier partners — your name, address and phone number, only to deliver your order.
  • Razorpay — to process payments, under their own privacy policy.
  • Google Firebase and Cloudflare — our hosting and login providers, which store data on our behalf.
  • Authorities — when required by law.

4. Data security and retention

Your data is stored with Google Firebase using industry-standard security, and access to customer data is limited to our authorised staff. We keep order records for as long as needed for delivery, returns and legal or tax requirements, and account data until you ask us to delete it.

5. Your choices

You can view and update your saved addresses and name in “My Account”. To access, correct or delete your personal data, email us at parvezmalik0627@gmail.com.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version.
